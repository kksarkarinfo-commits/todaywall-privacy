TodayWall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · বাংলা

English

TodayWall ("the app") is made by Bright Extend.

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

  • Your tasks, alarm times and settings are saved only on your own phone. They never leave it.
  • The app has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not use the internet (it does not even ask for the internet permission).
  • The app never changes your wallpaper and does not read your photos, contacts, location or files.
  • If you uninstall the app, all of its data is removed from your phone.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show the reminder when an alarm time you set is reached.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: so the reminder can vibrate.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
